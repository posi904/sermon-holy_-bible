Privacy Policy

Distraction-free Word for Believers.

No data collected · Works offline

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In plain words: Holy Bible is an offline Bible reader and sermon-note app. It does not use the internet, does not ask you to create an account, and does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information — not your name, not your email, not your device ID, and not your location.

Everything you create in the app (bookmarks, favourites, reading themes, reading progress and your Sermon Notes) stays in private storage on your own device. There is no server behind the app for it to talk to.

1. Who we are

Holy Bible (“the app”) is a free Android application published by its developer for readers, students and churches. Its purpose is to offer a calm, distraction-free place to read the Word of God and to write sermons. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. The app works fully offline

This is the single most important fact in this document. The app is designed to run without a network connection:

  • The Bible text in Telugu, English and Hindi is bundled inside the app and reads from on-device storage.
  • The app does not declare the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of connecting to a server.
  • Cleartext traffic is disabled and Android cloud backup is switched off.
  • You can use the full reading, study, notes and settings experience on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere with no signal.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, rent or trade any personal user data. Specifically, the app never asks for, accesses or has access to:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Device or advertising ID
  • GPS / precise or coarse location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Microphone or camera
  • Call logs or SMS
  • Files outside the app sandbox
  • Health or fitness data
  • Payment details

There is no sign-up, no login, no password and no user account. Because we operate no servers and no accounts, there is no profile about you anywhere.

Android permissions: the app requests no runtime permissions at all. There is no internet, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, location or phone permission in the app manifest.

4. What is stored — and where

The app saves only what you actively create or choose, using a private SQLite database and preferences held in the app’s own sandbox directory on your device. None of it leaves your phone.

5. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no third-party advertising networks and no analytics or tracking SDKs. There is no Google Ads, AdMob, Facebook SDK, Firebase Analytics, App Center, Crashlytics, or any comparable telemetry, attribution or fingerprinting library embedded in the app.

  • No ads are shown — not banner, not interstitial, not sponsored results.
  • No analytics events, screen-view reports, sessions or usage statistics are recorded or sent anywhere.
  • No advertising ID is read, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking or fingerprinting occurs.
  • Your reading habits are not profiled, and the app contains nothing that would allow it to be.
  • The app is entirely free and shows no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content.

The only third-party code inside the app is a handful of standard, permissively-licensed open-source software libraries used to build the interface and store data locally (for example Flutter). None of them transmit your data.

6. Sharing, export and ratings

Sharing only ever happens when you explicitly choose it. The app can hand a passage, a verse or a sermon note to another app through Android’s normal share sheet. That text goes directly from your device to the app you picked; we are not involved and we never receive it. The same is true of the “Rate the app” button, which simply opens your phone’s link to the Google Play listing. We do not receive reviews or ratings through any private channel.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you are in complete control of it:

  • You can delete an individual Sermon Note, bookmark or favourite from within the app at any time.
  • You can clear reading history and reset your settings from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its local data. There is no cloud copy and no backup we could restore it from, so please export or re-type anything you want to keep before uninstalling.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request.

8. Children’s privacy

Holy Bible is suitable for readers of all ages, including children and teenagers. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone — adults or children — there is no special handling to describe. We never know who is using the app, and a child’s notes are protected exactly as an adult’s are: on their own device, and nowhere else.

9. Security

Privacy by architecture is the strongest form of security, and it is the one we have chosen:

  • No network stack means no transmission, no interception, no breach surface.
  • Data is confined to the app’s private sandbox, which is isolated from other apps and requires the device unlock to access in normal use.
  • Android cloud backup and cleartext traffic are disabled for the app.
  • Uninstalling removes the data permanently.

Please note that we cannot recover anything for you: because we never receive a copy, we also have no way to restore it.

10. Scripture text and app content

The Bible translations included in the app are in the public domain or are made freely available by their publishers. The app’s interface text, icons and design are the property of the developer. Reading and using the app grants no rights to the underlying text beyond your own fair personal use. Please do not republish the translations as part of another product without checking the terms that apply to them.

11. Your sermon notes

Your notes are your own. They are private to you, are not shared with any other user, are not shown to the developer, and are not used for any purpose. Because they never leave your device, we are also not responsible for their loss — please keep your own copy of anything important.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change and the revised policy will be published at this same address. Any material change will be announced in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
